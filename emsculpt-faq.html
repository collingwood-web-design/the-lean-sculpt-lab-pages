About the Treatment

How does EMSCULPT NEO® work?

EMSCULPT NEO® is powered by an innovative applicator that simultaneously delivers synchronized RF and HIFEM+ energies. The radiofrequency heating rapidly elevates muscle temperature by several degrees, effectively preparing it for stress, much like a warm-up before any workout. In under four minutes, the temperature in subcutaneous fat reaches levels that trigger apoptosis, permanently damaging fat cells which are then gradually eliminated from the body. Clinical studies indicate an average reduction of 30% in subcutaneous fat. By bypassing the brain’s limitations, HIFEM+ energy stimulates 100% of the muscle fibers in the targeted area at intensities unattainable through voluntary exercise. This extreme stress compels the muscles to adapt, leading to an increase in both the number and size of muscle fibers and cells, with an average muscle volume increase of 25%.

Does EMSCULPT NEO® really work?

Yes! EMSCULPT NEO® and the embedded high intensity magnetic field (HIFEM) technology has been clinically tested for safety and efficacy through seven clinical studies*. Moreover, 30 scientific publications make HIFEM the most intensively researched body contouring technology since its introduction in 2018.

How is EMSCULPT NEO® procedure performed?

EMSCULPT NEO® procedure is straightforward and requires no prior preparation. You will simply lie down as the applicators are positioned over the treatment area for 30 minutes. Throughout the session, you may experience intense yet manageable muscle contractions accompanied by a warming sensation reminiscent of a hot stone massage. Once the treatment concludes, you can seamlessly resume your daily activities.

What is the protocol for EMSCULPT NEO® ?

The typical protocol for EMSCULPT NEO® involves a series of four treatments, each lasting 30 minutes and spaced 5 to 10 days apart.

Unlike liposuction, which involves invasive surgery, EMSCULPT NEO® builds muscle while simultaneously reducing fat, making it an effective and non-invasive alternative for those looking to enhance their body contouring results.

Can EMSCULPT NEO® be used by slim patients for muscle building?

Even slimmer patients interested in muscle building can experience significant advantages when utilizing EMSCULPT NEO®, with clinical studies revealing an average increase of 25% in muscle mass.

No, EMSCULPT NEO® procedure does not help with weight reduction. The therapy helps reduce your fat and build your muscles.

After the treatment, the dead fat cells are naturally eliminated from the body through metabolic processes, ensuring that the remaining substances are also effectively processed and removed.

Your Treatment Experience

How to prepare for your EMSCULPT NEO® appointment?

Please ensure you are well-hydrated the day before your treatment and wear comfortable clothing. Your skin needs to be clean, and the applicator should lie flat against it, as it cannot be applied over clothing. If you have any abdominal, nipple, or genital piercings, please remove them beforehand, as they can heat up during the procedure and potentially cause burns. Additionally, please take off your watch, belt, and any electronic devices, such as your phone, to prevent overheating or malfunctioning. You may keep your earrings, necklaces, and bracelets on, but you are welcome to remove them if you prefer.

Are there any supplements or diets I need to follow?

EMSCULPT NEO® treatment does not require any pre-treatment preparations, allowing you to return to your regular lifestyle immediately after the procedure. For optimal results, it is advisable to maintain a healthy lifestyle, balanced diet, and proper hydration.

What to expect during EMSCULPT NEO® treatment

You will experience a gentle, warming sensation during the first 3 to 4 minutes, after which the focus will shift to the contractions in your abdomen, making the heat sensation less noticeable, similar to a hot stone massage. The temperature within the applicator is continuously monitored and features an automatic shut-off when it exceeds 44 degrees Celsius, with the optimal temperature for lipolysis or fat cell destruction being between 42 and 44 degrees Celsius.

What to expect after EMSCULPT NEO® treatment

There are no marks left on the skin after the procedure; any redness caused by the heat typically fades within about an hour. You may experience mild tenderness or soreness, but that is the extent of it. Expect no recovery time, no pain, and no downtime, with absolutely no restrictions.

Is there any downtime?

The EMSCULPT NEO® is non-invasive and requires no recovery time. Please hydrate the day before treatment and wear comfortable clothing.

What are the side effects?

The most frequently experienced side effects include tenderness and soreness in the treated area; immediately afterward, your skin may feel warm, but that’s the extent of it, with virtually no visible signs of any procedure having taken place.

How fast will I see results?

Each patient’s results may vary, but typically the most significant changes are noticeable around three months following the final treatment.

Safety & Eligibility

Am I a candidate?

We will arrange an initial consultation with our physician to identify the most effective approach.

Can EMSCULPT NEO® be done on a darker skin colour?

Yes

Can I get EMSCULPT NEO® treatment if I have an insulin pump?

If you are able to easily remove your insulin pump for the 30-minute duration of the treatment, then you should be eligible for EMSCULPT NEO therapy, but this will be confirmed during your initial consultation with our physician.

Can I get EMSCULPT NEO® treatment if I have an IUD? What about copper IUD?

This topic will be discussed with our physician during your consultation. You should not use Emsculpt Neo® if you have a copper IUD.

Can I get EMSCULPT NEO® treatment if I have dental implants?

Yes, it is safe to receive EMSculpt Neo® treatment when you have dental implants.

Can I get EMSCULPT NEO® treatment if I have had hip or knee replacement?

It’s a great question, but the answer is simple: it depends on each individual, the type of hardware used, and whether their orthopedic surgeon or neurosurgeon has approved them for it. A careful examination and in-person consultation with our physician are necessary. Generally, hip replacement would be contraindicated.

Can I get EMSCULPT NEO® treatment if I have metal hardware in my spine?

It’s not a simple yes or no answer; rather, it depends. You should discuss it with your spine surgeon. If you have previously undergone an MRI on your back, this should not pose an issue, as the MRI magnet is much stronger than the HIFEM magnet used in EMSculpt Neo®. Our in-house physician will guide you.

Can I get EMSCULPT NEO® treatment if I am pregnant?

Absolutely not. This treatment has not been studied, and our physician does not feel it is appropriate or ethical to administer it even in areas other than the abdomen.

Can I get EMSCULPT NEO® treatment while breastfeeding?

There is no solid consensus on this topic, but our in-house physician feels comfortable recommending EMSCULPT NEO treatment to his breastfeeding patients. The details of this can be discussed during your in-person consultation.

Can I get EMSCULPT NEO® treatment after surgery?

It is always best to consult with your surgeon before undergoing any treatments in the area where surgery was performed. While it’s impossible to address every surgery or treatment area, if you are considering treatments for the abdominal area and have recently undergone a hernia repair, tummy tuck, appendectomy, or C-section, it is advisable to wait 3 to 6 months post-surgery before proceeding with this treatment.

Can I get EMSCULPT NEO® after a C-section?

It is advisable to wait about three months after your surgery to allow your uterus to return to its normal size before receiving EMSculpt treatment. While HIFEM technology does not affect internal muscles like the uterus, it is a safe precaution based purely on physiological science to wait about three months to avoid any unnecessary uterine stimulation. Yes, you can get EMSculpt Neo after having a C-section, but it should not be applied directly over your scar, as it may cause a blister.

Can I get EMSCULPT NEO® if I have a tattoo?

If you have a tattoo in the area where the applicator will be applied, you are not a candidate for EMSculpt Neo® treatment. The radio frequency component of the applicator may cause blistering of the skin, as the tattooing process involves micro scarring to deliver the ink beneath the skin.
