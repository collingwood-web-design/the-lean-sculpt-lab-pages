Diet Plan

A medically supervised weight loss program crafted by physicians provides a thorough and supportive framework that empowers individuals to reach and maintain their ideal weight in a healthy manner.

Initial Assessment:

The program begins with a comprehensive evaluation by a physician, which may include a review of the individual’s medical history, current health status, lifestyle habits, and any underlying conditions such as diabetes, hypertension, or thyroid disorders. The doctor may also conduct physical exams and order lab tests to assess overall health.

Weight Loss Program

Personalized Plan:

Based on the assessment, the physician will create a customized weight loss plan tailored to the individual’s specific needs. This plan may include dietary recommendations, exercise routines, behavior modification strategies, and, if necessary, medications or supplements to aid in weight loss.

Nutrition Guidance:

The program offers a balanced and nutrient-dense eating plan aimed at fostering healthy weight loss. This plan emphasizes portion control, calorie reduction, macronutrient balance, including protein, fats, and carbohydrates, as well as the incorporation of whole foods. Additionally, it takes into consideration any dietary restrictions or preferences.

Exercise Recommendations:

A physician-approved exercise regimen will be an integral part of the program, emphasizing physical activity that is both safe and effective for the individual. This may encompass a combination of aerobic, strength training, and flexibility exercises designed specifically to align with the person’s fitness level and goals.

Behavioral Support:

To help individuals make lasting changes, the program offers behavioral therapy or counseling that addresses emotional eating, stress management, and motivation, enabling individuals to build healthier habits and foster a positive relationship with food and exercise.

Regular Monitoring

A vital aspect of the program is the physician’s ongoing supervision. The doctor regularly monitors progress through follow-up visits, weigh-ins, lab tests, and body composition measurements. This process allows for necessary adjustments to the plan and ensures that individuals are progressing safely and effectively.

Education and Support:

The program may include educational resources on nutrition, exercise, and strategies for maintaining long-term weight loss, with support groups or one-on-one counseling available to provide added encouragement and accountability.

Weight Loss Medication

Ozempic / Wegovy / Saxenda / Mounjaro

GLP-1 Medications:

A weight loss program using GLP-1 (glucagon-like peptide-1) receptor agonists, such as Ozempic / Wegovy / Saxenda / Mounjaro combines medication with lifestyle changes to support sustainable weight loss. GLP-1 medications work by mimicking a natural hormone that helps regulate appetite, promote satiety, and slow gastric emptying, making it easier for individuals to consume fewer calories without feeling excessively hungry. The program typically includes a personalized exercise plan, incorporating both aerobic and strength-training activities to enhance calorie burning, improve muscle mass, and boost metabolism. Additionally, dietary modifications focus on nutrient-dense, lower-calorie foods, which, combined with the appetite-suppressing effects of GLP-1, help individuals stick to a calorie deficit. Regular follow-ups with a healthcare provider ensure the program’s effectiveness and allow for adjustments based on progress and individual response. This integrated approach, with the support of GLP-1 therapy, not only promotes weight loss but also improves metabolic health, reduces the risk of chronic diseases, and supports long-term lifestyle changes.
