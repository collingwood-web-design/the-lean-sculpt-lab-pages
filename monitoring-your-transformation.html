How We Monitor Your Transformation

3D Body Scanner

At The Lean Sculpt Lab we use Styku® 3D body scanner to accurately monitor your progress.

What is a 3D body scanner?

A 3D body scanner, as the name suggests, scans your body using infrared technology. It captures hundreds of photos and thousands of point locations to stitch them together into a point cloud, creating a uniquely individual 3D model of your body. In the realm of fitness, this data provides precise measurements of an individual’s waist, hips, thighs, arms, chest, and height, while also tracking specific weight and its distribution across the body. These scans offer greater accuracy compared to the commonly used Bioelectrical Impedance Analysis, or BIA, which measures the electrical current flow through the body to determine body composition and fat. Also, BIA results can be misleading due to natural variations in factors like water intake and food consumption before testing.
