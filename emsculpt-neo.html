What is EMSCULPT NEO®

How does it work?

Experience the future of body sculpting at The Lean Sculpt Lab with EMSCULPT NEO®. The first non-invasive body sculpting device that helps eliminate fat cells and build muscle in a 30-minute treatment session. The treatment combines Radiofrequency heating and the HIFEM™ procedure to reduce fat and build muscle. This is a permanent fat reduction!

A 30 minute EMSCULPT NEO® treatment is the equivalent of either 20,000 sit ups or 20,000 squats while laying down.

Fat Reduction:

EMSCULPT NEO® can reduce fat by up to 30% in the treated areas, making it highly effective for body contouring.

Quick and Non-Invasive:

Each session lasts only 30 minutes, with no downtime required, allowing you to resume your daily activities immediately.

EMSCULPT NEO® delivers RF energy to heat your muscles, mimicking a warm-up before a workout. Then, the HIFEM energy induces powerful muscle contractions—much more powerful than what you get with standard exercise.

The result?

Significant muscle growth and rapid fat loss.
